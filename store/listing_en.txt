QRow: QR Scanner to Table

SHORT DESCRIPTION (up to 80 characters)
Scan QR codes into a table and export to Excel

FULL DESCRIPTION (up to 4000 characters)
QRow turns your phone camera into a fast data collector. Point it at QR codes and the app puts everything into a tidy table that you can export to Excel with one tap.

Great for inventory, equipment and goods tracking, event check-in, invoice checks, and any task where you need to scan many codes in a row.

FEATURES
• Fast scanning: codes are recognized instantly, no button press needed
• Records table: number, content, code type, date and time
• One-tap export to Excel (.xlsx): send the file by email, messenger or cloud drive
• Flashlight for dark places
• Smooth zoom slider for distant or small codes
• Duplicate protection: the same code twice in a row is saved once
• Supports QR and popular barcodes (EAN, Code 128, Code 39, UPC, Data Matrix, PDF417, Aztec)
• Tap a record to copy its content
• Modern interface with dark theme

PRIVACY
Your data stays on your device. No sign-up, no ads, and your records are never uploaded to a server. The camera is used only to read codes.

HOW TO USE
1. Allow camera access.
2. Point the camera at a QR code: it appears in the table.
3. Open the Table tab and tap "Export to Excel".
